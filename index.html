Walt Disney Concert Hall

2026–27 Season Listening Guide

135 classical concerts · October 2026 – May 2027

Every concert is listed under its date with its full program. Blue entries are clickable links to YouTube videos of each piece.

The videos are representative recordings by leading orchestras, soloists, and choirs — not the LA Phil’s own performances. The artists named on each concert are who you’ll hear at Disney Hall; the gray note names the recording you’ll hear on YouTube.

Six film-with-orchestra screenings (Home Alone, Elf, Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde, Werewolf by Night) are omitted as non-classical. A handful of concerts had no published program yet; those appear with their date and artists but no links.

The 2026/27 Disney Hall season ends May 30, 2027 — nothing is scheduled for June 2027.
